🔬 Details for physicists ▶

CERN TT20 Transfer Line: 400 GeV/c slow-extracted proton beam from SPS to North Area target over 900 meters. Uncoupled linear optics with 10 horizontal / 12 vertical corrector dipoles and 11 horizontal / 10 vertical split-foil secondary emission monitors (SEMs). Uniform background noise ν = 0.25, Table 1 medium optics randomization. The Bayesian benchmark uses a 100k-particle filter (Adam Štafa CERN report, 2026).

Plot: the tube is the aperture profile a(s) at 5 m checkpoints; the beam is the true closed trajectory x(s) = R(Δθ − gθ) on the perturbed machine (god view, never shown to the agent). Magnet arrows show the commanded push −θ (the hidden offset Δθ is not drawn). Sensor rings are coloured by the true normalised symmetry Ŝ (green ≥ 0.9); the small dot inside each ring is the beam offset magnified to ±1.5σ at the ring edge. Tooltip plate bars are the noisy foil readings I₁ = r·ℓ, I₂ = (1−r)·ℓ.
